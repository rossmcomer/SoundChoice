# SoundChoice DJ Services Booking App
## Event booking app with admin console, Google calendar, e-mail verification, social media feed, Stripe for payments

Visit the app at https://rosscomerdemo.com

### VIEW INTRO VIDEO, ABOUT SECTION, TESTIMONIALS, PRICING PAGES

### HOW TO SIGN UP

To book an event you need to start by visiting the login/signup page and clicking the Sign up! button.  After submitting your account info you will recieve an e-mail from SoundChoice Support to verify your e-mail.  Click the link, then return to the login page to sign in.

### HOW TO BOOK AN EVENT

You must sign in with an account to book an event.  Upon signing in you can click the "Book Now!" button to book an event.  Pick a date, location, start time, total hours, and whether you would like to purchase additional uplighting.  Scroll down to click the "Proceed to Checkout" button.  This will take you to the Stripe page where you can simulate a payment.

### SIMULATE PAYMENT

To simulate a successful payment, use credit card number "4242424242424242".  After payment is confirmed you will be redirected to the success page.

To simulate a declined payment, use credit card number "4000000000009995".  After payment is declined you will be redirected to the payment cancelled page.

### SUBMIT EVENT DETAILS

After completing your payment, visit your Profile page by clicking the "Profile" button in the hamburger menu.  On your profile page you can fill out the required questionnaire for your event, pay the second half of your event's fee, and view your event/payment info.
